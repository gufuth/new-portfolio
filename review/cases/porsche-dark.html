Porsche × Lucasfilm

The Designer Alliance
IanCopy and associate creative direction

Porsche built a spaceship.

Idea

Build something in it.

Do not advertise next to Star Wars.

What it proved

1.5 m

A real, 1.5-meter S-91x, unveiled alongside The Rise of Skywalker.

Two design cultures made one real object together. The campaign followed the build instead of borrowing the franchise.

Assignment

Earn Porsche a credible place inside Star Wars without becoming another logo next to the franchise.

Porsche designers and Lucasfilm starship artists designed the S-91x together. The campaign documented the collaboration and unveiled the finished object at the world premiere.

What mattered

The work earned credibility by making the collaboration tangible before asking anyone to care about the campaign around it.
