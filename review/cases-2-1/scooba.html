SCOOBA LOVE

Love the idea. Show the math.

A shared framework for evaluating creative work without sanding the life out of it.

Format  Internal creative systemRole  Concept + script developmentTeam  Katerina, Weston, Ian

What happened

Passion makes it easy to defend an idea before proving it. SCOOBA LOVE gave the team a shared set of criteria for creating and evaluating work. The point was simple: keep the passion, but be able to explain why the idea works.

This is not client campaign work. It is evidence of how Ian thinks about the work: concept, language, critique and a system that helps a creative team make better decisions together.

System · How to evaluate work

The questions

  1. Is it on Strategy?
  2. Is it the best work to achieve the Client’s Goal?
  3. Is it Ownable?
  4. Can you summarize it in One sentence?
  5. Why would you Buy it?
  6. Is it Achievable?
  7. Why do you LOVE it?
