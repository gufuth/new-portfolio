Outdoor Voices

Everyone was ripping off their leggings. Literally.

Role  Social strategy, copy, TOVFormat  Caption systems, tone rules

What happened

The product was easy to copy and the social voice had become scattered. If the product could be imitated, the voice could not afford to be generic.

The feed shifted toward people actually moving, with a voice built around recreation instead of performance culture. Caption systems and tone rules made the channel recognizably Outdoor Voices again.
