What happened

The first fully crewed commercial spaceflight was going to happen once. Virgin Galactic needed the world to care at the exact same time.

The pre-launch narrative treated space as a shared human moment rather than a story for enthusiasts. The work included voice guidelines, countdown content and livestream promotion across the major social platforms.
