Jose Cuervo

Make 250 years feel current.

Role  Creative strategy, copy, video directionFormat  Social, video and experiential

What happened

Launch two new products in categories obsessed with whatever is newest in the room. Use the authority Cuervo already had. Change the pace, format and occasion around it.

Playamar. A new tequila-based drink needed pace, appetite and social relevance without making Cuervo pretend it had just arrived. The launch changed the occasion and the behavior around the brand, not the brand’s age.

Tradicional Cristalino. The second launch shifted the visual temperature and drinking occasion while keeping the same principle: use heritage as authority, then make the surrounding behavior feel current.

Nobody talks about the 250-year-old at the party.
