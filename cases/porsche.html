Porsche × Lucasfilm · The Designer Alliance

In 2019, Porsche built a spaceship. This is not a metaphor.

ROLE · Copy, ACD
The problem

Brand collaborations usually mean two logos sharing an object. Porsche needed a way into Star Wars that both fanbases could take seriously.

The idea

Don’t advertise next to Star Wars. Build something in it.

The work

Porsche designers and Lucasfilm starship artists co-designed the Tri-Wing S-91x Pegasus Starfighter. The campaign documented the process and launched the finished ship alongside The Rise of Skywalker.

Proof
S-91x unveiled alongside The Rise of Skywalker world premiere
Physical 1.5m model built as part of the collaboration
