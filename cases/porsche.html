The problem

Brand collaborations usually stop at adjacency. Two names share the same object, campaign or media moment. Porsche needed a reason to exist inside Star Wars that both design audiences and fans could take seriously.

The move

Porsche designers and Lucasfilm starship artists worked together on the thing itself. The campaign followed that process, then launched the finished S-91x as the proof.
