Assignment

Earn Porsche a credible place inside Star Wars without becoming another logo next to the franchise.

The move

Porsche designers and Lucasfilm starship artists designed the S-91x together. The campaign documented that collaboration and unveiled the finished object at the world premiere.

Ian

Copy and associate creative direction across the collaboration story and campaign expression.

The idea, in one line
Build something in it.
What mattered

The partnership produced a 1.5-meter physical S-91x and unveiled it alongside The Rise of Skywalker. The work earned credibility by making the collaboration tangible before asking anyone to care about the campaign around it.
