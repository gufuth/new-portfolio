Selsun Blue · Dan Driff

I wrote dialogue for dandruff. The MFA finally paid off.

ROLE · Copy, character development
The problem

Dandruff shampoo is a forgettable category full of lab coats and falling-snow metaphors. Selsun Blue needed something younger people could actually remember.

The idea

Dan Driff, the world’s worst wingman.

The work

Dandruff became a character: the guy who appears at the worst possible moment and never realizes he is the problem. The character carried TV and digital spots without turning the campaign into another claims demo.

Character platform extended into additional pre-roll films
