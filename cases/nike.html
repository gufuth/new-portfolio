Nike SB × Staple · Panda Pigeon

In 2005, this sneaker started a riot. Nike asked if it still could.

The problem

Reissuing a legend is a trap. Play the nostalgia card and the community that made the shoe will call you a tourist.

The work

Three sample colorways went out into the sneaker community. The black-and-white won. The campaign then paid tribute to the history behind the Pigeon Dunk, including Jeff Staple’s Chinatown roots and newspaper coverage from the original 2005 release.
