The tension
Nobody talks about the 250-year-old at the party.

The line is a door into the case, not the case itself.

Chapter 01

Playamar

A new product needed pace, appetite and social relevance without throwing away the weight of the parent brand. The work changed the format around Cuervo instead of dressing Cuervo up as something it was not.

Chapter 02

Tradicional Cristalino

The second launch shifted the visual temperature and occasion, but kept the same governing principle. New behavior around an old name, not a costume change for the old name.

What stayed constant

The relationship covered different products, channels and occasions. The through-line was restraint: use the authority Cuervo already had, then change the pace, format and behavior around it.
