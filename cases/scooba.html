What it was

SCOOBA LOVE gave the team a shared set of criteria for creating and evaluating work. The point was simple: keep the passion, but be able to explain why the idea works.

The questions

Is it on Strategy? Is it the best work to achieve the Client’s Goal? Is it Ownable? Can you summarize it in One sentence? Why would you Buy it? Is it Achievable? Why do you LOVE it?

7Questions force subjective creative debate into a shared language.
1Common standard for deciding whether an idea deserves more love.
Creative direction
There are millions of ideas in the sea.

The source scripts used divers, whales, treasure, bad fish and other deliberately overcommitted ocean logic to make the system memorable while keeping the evaluation criteria intact.

Why it belongs here

This is not client campaign work. It is evidence of how Ian thinks about the work: concept, language, critique and a system that helps a creative team make better decisions together.
