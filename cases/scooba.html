Internal creative system · SCOOBA LOVE

Love the idea. Show the math.

A shared framework for evaluating creative work without sanding the life out of it.

IAN · CONCEPT + SCRIPT DEVELOPMENT · WITH KATERINA + WESTON
Problem

Creative reviews get subjective fast. Passion makes it easy to defend an idea before proving why it works.

SystemGive the team a shared set of questions without pretending creativity is mathematical.
The framework
  1. 01Is it on Strategy?
  2. 02Is it the best work to achieve the Client’s Goal?
  3. 03Is it Ownable?
  4. 04Can you summarize it in One sentence?
  5. 05Why would you Buy it?
  6. 06Is it Achievable?
  7. 07Why do you LOVE it?

The point was not to score creativity. It was to make the argument around an idea clearer, earlier, and shared by the people in the room.

From the scripts
There are millions of ideas in the sea.

The source scripts used divers, whales, treasure, bad fish and other deliberately overcommitted ocean logic to make the framework memorable while keeping the evaluation criteria intact.

Why it belongs here

This is not client campaign work and it does not need a fake results section. It is evidence of how Ian thinks about creative work: concept, language, critique and a system for making better decisions together.
