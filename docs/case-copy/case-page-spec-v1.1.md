## 0. The taste consultation

**Refused:** atmospheric reading surfaces; winking (no "isn't our diner theme fun" moments — and no costuming the work itself); declared claims instead of shown objects; over-explained copy; over-polished smoothness.

**Surviving quality nodes:** PROOF THROUGH OBJECT (the page is an artifact; the work is shown as objects that argue for themselves) · COMPRESSED DIRECTNESS (type is the move; two display moments; 300-word ceiling; figures without adjectives) · HUMAN TEXTURE OVER SMOOTHNESS (paper grain + slight misregistration; that's the whole texture budget — no coffee rings, no kitsch).

Anchors: Delcan (printed matter as emotional register), CraigWard (letterforms carry everything), Mills (documentary witness, honest print). Function in, costume out.

## 1. The core move

Clicking a sign pushes you INTO the sign (~400ms camera push through the sign face); the case page is the lit sign's paper at full bleed. Cream field, near-black ink, the dark world surviving only at the edges: pinned slugline strip on top, letterboxed margins, dark footer. Frame counter increments, clock keeps running, and the page never references the diner. It just obeys its physics.

## 2. Design system

- **Grid:** one centered column, 680–720px text measure; media breaks wider, full-bleed within the paper.
- **Type:** two faces total. The site's mono for the institutional layer (sluglines, labels, context strip, captions, sourcing lines, credits); one display grotesque (the sign lettering face) for exactly two moments — hook and idea line — plus the stat figures. Body in the mono, 16–18px, 1.6 line height.
- **Color:** sign-cream paper, near-black ink, ONE accent with ONE job (idea line or stat figures, constant across all cases), pulled from the world's existing neon.
- **Texture:** paper grain + a hair of print misregistration on display type. Nothing else.
- **Media:** films and stills mandatory and central — plain, full-width, like photographs tipped into a document, each with a mono caption (EXHIBIT A — :30 SPOT). No CRT frames, no polaroid borders.
- **Motion:** the push-in transition and a hover flicker on the booth signs. Nothing else.

## 3. Layout — the scroll order (~300 words + media)

1. **SLUGLINE STRIP** (dark, pinned): CASE 01 — BEAST GAMES — AMAZON PRIME VIDEO · FRAME 004 + clock.
2. **THE HOOK** — marquee display type, the deadpan flat statement.
3. **CONTEXT STRIP** — mono small caps: CLIENT · YEAR · AGENCY · ROLE: ACD, COPY LEAD. Role clarity in the first screen.
4. **HERO MEDIA** — film or key asset immediately. Work before words, no exceptions.
5. **THE PROBLEM** — mono label, 2–3 sentences.
6. **THE IDEA** — second and last display moment, accent color, one line, named.
7. **THE WORK** — 2–3 sentences + the exhibit band (stills/spots with mono captions).
8. **THE COPY** — campaign lines shown as artifacts, not claimed line-by-line. Wherever possible the assets carry the copy (the billboard photo shows the headline). Where a line matters and no asset survives, a plain pull-quote labeled by channel only: OOH: / PUSH: / SOCIAL:. No authorship tags on individual lines — the context strip already says "wrote most of the campaign copy," the honest, defensible claim for years-old work. On thin-asset cases this section shrinks or disappears; the media band absorbs it.
9. **THE NUMBERS** — 3–4 stat blocks: big display figure + mono sourcing line each, then the attribution sentence in mono italic. No adjectives near figures.
10. **CREDITS** — end titles: two-column mono small caps, ROLE / NAME.
11. **LAST CALL** — the takeaway, one sentence, mono. (Fallback label if it ever feels cute: "THE POINT".)
12. **FOOTER** (dark): NEXT SIGN → GO / ESPN · The counter's open: Ianr.luna@gmail.com.

**Skim test:** bold/display elements alone deliver hook → idea → figures → role.

**Anti-generic move:** the template doesn't need an invented per-page flourish. Into-the-sign IS the move at template level; inside the paper, restraint is the move. A second one would be stacking.

## 4. Copy register per section

- **Hook:** flat statement, absurdity unacknowledged, never selling.
- **Labels:** the institutional mono voice IS the deadpan — THE PROBLEM, THE IDEA, THE WORK, THE COPY, THE NUMBERS, CREDITS. The labels are the full extent of in-world dressing on content; the work itself is never costumed.
- **Body:** the written 300-word case copy; forward pressure between sections.
- **Numbers:** figure + source + timeframe, always.
- **Mobile:** the single column IS the mobile design; slugline pinned, marquee scales, exhibits stack.

## 5. Consistency rule

Same paper, same two faces, same accent, same section order on every case; only content density varies (more exhibits on Nike, the films carrying Selsun, more numbers on MrBeast). A system that holds across ten cases reads as judgment; ten art-directed one-offs read as indecision. The world can be theatrical because the documents inside it are ruthlessly consistent.
