## How to read this document

Every case study below is rewritten to the standard spec (hook → context strip → problem → named idea → the work and the lines → numbers → credit → takeaway).

**About the numbers.** Anything in **[brackets] is a placeholder**: an invented but feasible figure, scaled to what that project actually was, shown so you can see the type, size, and sourcing format reviewers expect. Do not ship a bracket. Swap in your real data, calibrated against a named benchmark or expressed as a range; if the real number is smaller than the placeholder, the number wins, never the narrative. Numbers marked **(real)** are already public or already claimed on your site and just need a source attached.

**About the sample lines.** Sections labeled "Lines I wrote" show placeholder examples of what that section looks like. Replace with lines you actually shipped. This section matters more than the metrics: it's the only place a copywriter's book shows the product.

# Giving away $4.2 million? That was the easy part.
