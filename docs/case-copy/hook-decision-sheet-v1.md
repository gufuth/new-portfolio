**Date:** 2026-07-06 · Companion to Case Study Rewrites v1.1 (hooks unchanged there until you pick) **Register rule applied:** hooks never sell, they tell. Flat statement, absurdity unacknowledged, underwhelmed delivery carries the joke. My lean is one editor's opinion; your ear wins.

**1. MrBeast x MoneyLion**
- Current: "Giving away $4.2 million? That was the easy part."
- Alt: **"We gave away $4.2 million. The hard part was the push notifications."**
- Lean: current is good (it's built from your own "Pfft, that's easy"), but the alt places you smaller and funnier. Coin flip.

**2. Virgin Galactic**
- Current: "One launch. One chance. Five million people, or it didn't count."
- Alt: **"Richard Branson went to space. Somebody had to tell everyone."**
- Lean: alt. Current is trailer-voice, the one hook that argues hardest.

**3. Nike SB**
- Current: "In 2005, this sneaker started a riot. Nike asked if it still could."
- Alt: **"The last time this sneaker dropped, there was a riot. No pressure."**
- Lean: keep current. It's factual, not salesy, and strong. The alt echoes the "No pressure, right? Ehem." beat already on your live page, so it's there if you want the callback.

**4. Outdoor Voices**
- Current: "Everyone was ripping off their leggings. Literally."
- Alt: **"The leggings got knocked off weekly. Nobody managed to knock off the captions."**
- Lean: current is good; "Literally." is a pun-tag, slightly winkier than your register, but it lands. Alt is flatter and sets up your job. Coin flip.

**5. Jose Cuervo**
- Current: "Nobody talks about the 250-year-old at the party."
- Alt: **"Jose Cuervo is 250 years old. The seltzer drinkers were not impressed."**
- Lean: keep current. Best hook in the doc: flat, unexplained, no sell. Alt exists because you asked.

**6. The Atlantic**
- Current: "165 years of journalism. A feed that read like homework."
- Alt: **"The Atlantic has been publishing since 1857. Instagram hadn't heard."**
- Lean: coin flip. Current is tight; alt trades the constructed contrast for the underwhelmed-reaction shape and a real date.

**7. 1800 Tequila**
- Current: "The trophies were real. The swagger wasn't making it to the shelf."
- Alt: **"1800 is the most awarded tequila on Earth. It kept mentioning that."**
- Lean: alt. Current is a built antithesis; the alt makes the brand's insecurity the joke, which is also the case's actual insight.

**8. Silvercar by Audi**
- Current: "A rental car company with no counters, no lines, and one story worth telling."
- Alt: **"Silvercar had no counters, no lines, no paperwork. That left the car."**
- Lean: alt. "One story worth telling" is the hook selling itself.

**9. car2go x Tinder**
- Current: "We bought ads on Tinder. The car did better than most of the guys on there."
- Alt: **"We put a car on Tinder. It matched."**
- Lean: keep current, it's already you. But the alt is the flattest sentence in this whole project and I'd regret not showing it to you.

**10. SCOOBA Love**
- Current: "Creatives grade their own homework. We built a system to make them stop."
- Alt: **"SCOOBA Love is a system for judging creative work. Yes, we named it that."**
- Lean: alt. "Yes, we named it that." does the self-aware-without-apologizing thing your testimonials page does.

**11. Vans**
- Current: "Everyone's first cool shoe. The job: keep it everyone's."
- Alt: **"Vans has been cool since 1966. We were asked not to ruin it."**
- Lean: alt. Current is a constructed mirror; the alt is a flat admission with a real date.

**12. Channel Craft** — "Don Draper never had to do any of this." No alt. It's already yours; touching it would be malpractice.

**Locked already (the new three, v1.1):** "In 2019, Porsche built a spaceship. This is not a metaphor." / "The brief said: market a cyborg to people who build robots for a living. I read it twice." / "I wrote dialogue for dandruff. The MFA finally paid off."
