# PZ Portfolio Last Stop Diner — Visual Rules and Drift Guardrails

## Current target in one sentence

Same diner, seen from the inside: booth-height, looking straight through the front plate-glass window at an ordinary wet street where the selected case studies live on physical cream sign cabinets across the road.

## Lock list

- Head-on reverse angle is the active /work branch.
- Ordinary wet street, not mythic road.
- Physical cream sign cabinets inspired by the IAN LUNA sign.
- Beast Games as clear hero; Go, Hometown, and The Quiet Issue as readable supporting signs.
- Practical light only: diner pendant, streetlamp, sign lamps.
- Warm enough interior to read booth/table; not a black cave.
- Bottom-left nav built live in HTML/CSS.
- Previous soft-lock nav type direction: warmer, more inviting, less terminal.
- Faint green contamination only, with no letterforms.

## Kill list

- No readable WORK letters on /work.
- No W, no O, no word fragments, no reflected green typography.
- No baked nav in AI images.
- No sign title blocked by mullion, beam, reflection, or foreground object.
- No epic highway, desert, post-apocalyptic road, dramatic sky, or mythic billboard field.
- No diagonal road branch unless explicitly revived for comparison.
- No beautiful scene that breaks architecture.
- No texture democracy. Every surface should not be equally interesting.
- No cyber glow, terminal UI, underlines, or techy nav regression.

## Image-generation poison words

Avoid using these as the main engine: cinematic, moody, glowing, atmospheric, dramatic, neon, noir, surreal, epic, dystopian, apocalyptic, dreamlike, hyper-detailed, richly textured, ultra-polished, concept art.

## Better functional language

Use: available-light photograph, practical light, underexposed night, ordinary wet street, faded cream sign cabinet, dirty sodium amber, black-green shadows, soft halation, lower microcontrast, dead dark areas, imperfect blacks, restrained detail, photographed from booth height.

## Production build rules

- Generate/design a clean background plate, not final UI.
- Prefer blank or controlled sign cabinets in the plate.
- Composite sign faces separately for readable case titles and consistent type.
- Build bottom-left nav live in HTML/CSS.
- Add shared treatment layers: sign grime, lamp falloff, acrylic/yellowed cream, glass dirt, faint non-letter green contamination, mild halation, subtle grain.
- Keep flaws in objects and materials, not in usability.

## What not to do next

- Do not generate immediately in a fresh chat.
- Do not start from mood words.
- Do not fix the image by adding more atmosphere.
- Do not let the road become the subject.
- Do not preserve a pretty image if it is physically wrong.
- Do not accept readable WORK contamination.
- Do not ask AI to solve typography, UX, atmosphere, and architecture all at once.
