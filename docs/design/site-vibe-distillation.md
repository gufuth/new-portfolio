# SITE VIBE DISTILLATION v1 — 2026-07-11 (overnight run)
Distilled from walking all surfaces + master docs, for the premium-details pass.

## The vibe in one paragraph
A 35mm film you can walk around inside. Always night, always quiet, one locked camera per scene. The diner is real: light comes from fixtures, signs, and a TV; nothing glows for decoration. The words are dry and the world is haunted — the comedy and the melancholy come from the same deadpan. Nothing performs for the camera; the visitor discovers, the site never demonstrates. Restraint is the luxury. The letterbox bars are the projector; the browser tab is part of the film.

## What "premium" means HERE (filtered through house law)
- Premium = physical manners, not more atmosphere. Every light change traces to a source; every sound to a gesture or a door.
- Premium = thresholds, not transitions. This site CUTS like a film. No crossfade grammar, no View Transitions default morphs (considered 2026-07-11, REJECTED: fights the cut grammar; VT also has scar history with the bars — deployed_review 05-30 item 12).
- Premium = the world keeps existing when you're not looking (rare headlights, tab title that waits, clock in the bars).
- Premium = arrivals and departures are directed (exposure settles, bells with dents, text going silent during cuts).
- Premium = loading is a scene, not a spinner (plates resolve like exposure, never pop).
- Premium = nothing asks for attention twice; nothing loops; silence and stillness are the ground state.
