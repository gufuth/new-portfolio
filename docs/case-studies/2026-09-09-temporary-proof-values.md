## Why these proof types

Hiring research consistently rewards clear ownership, outcomes and business/user value over process theater. NN/g hiring research specifically emphasizes outcomes, knowing what to measure, and making portfolios scannable because hiring managers rarely read every word. Brand-lift research also shows that recall / awareness / consideration are valid outcomes for awareness work, while launch and performance campaigns should favor trials, adoption, installs, accounts or sales over generic reach.

Visual rule: **maximum three proof signals on the first screen.** One may be a verified contextual scale number; the others should prove result, reach, adoption or independent validation. Never turn the case into a KPI dashboard.

## First-screen hierarchy lock

1. Client / project
2. Governing idea / headline
3. Ian's role
4. Maximum three proof signals
5. Dominant image

The proof signals are not cards. They are an editorial evidence rail: large figure, thin rule, restrained label, generous darkness.

## Image-rhythm lock

- One dominant hero image.
- One evidence spine below it.
- Full-width image or one intentional two-up pair, never arbitrary floating sizes.
- If a case only has one good supporting image, show one good image.
- Do not repeat an image to fill space.
- Last Stop supplies the edit and frame; client work keeps its own palette.
