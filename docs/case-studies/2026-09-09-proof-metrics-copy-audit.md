# Last Stop case studies - proof metrics + copy audit

Status: working production guidance, 2026-09-09

This document defines which proof belongs on each case page, which numbers Ian should retrieve from old teammates/records, and the copy changes needed before final case adaptation.

## Recruiter rule

A case should make four things obvious very quickly:

1. What is the client/project?
2. What was the problem or governing idea?
3. What did Ian own?
4. What proves the work mattered?

Do not make every datum equally loud.

Preferred proof order, when the case supports it:

1. Business or behavior result: sales, installs, accounts, sell-through, trial, leads, conversion.
2. Brand/perception result: awareness lift, consideration lift, ad recall, sentiment.
3. Audience action: entries, votes, shares, saves, engagement, qualified visits, completion rate.
4. Reach/scale: viewers, impressions, organic reach, earned-media reach.
5. Independent validation: Cannes Lions Shortlist/Lion, Shorty, Webby, press recognition.
6. Tangible production proof: physical object, number of launches, markets, scale of execution.

A case does not need a fake KPI because another case has one. The proof must match the assignment.

## Placeholder law

Until Ian verifies a metric, the build may use a clearly marked private-preview placeholder such as `XXM+`, `+XX pts`, `XXK`, or `X teams`. It must never be presented as a factual result on production.

When a real number arrives, save its source/provenance with the case.

## Cannes / honors language

Cannes Lions' official judging language is Shortlist, Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Grand Prix. Do not write `nominated for a Silver Lion` unless a primary record proves that exact wording. If the work reached the first-round final list, use:

`HONORS · CANNES LIONS · SHORTLIST · [YEAR] · [CATEGORY]`

If it actually won Silver:

`HONORS · CANNES LIONS · SILVER LION · [YEAR] · [CATEGORY]`

Do not use a decorative ribbon. It reads like a portfolio template and spends too much visual energy on the badge. Last Stop treatment: a thin rule, small `HONORS` label, then the exact award in dirty cream / muted brass. One major honor may sit near the first-screen proof. Secondary honors go lower in the evidence ledger.

Known current evidence: Alita has `Shorty Awards Gold Honor · Business to Business` in the current case/source material. An older shared-work document mentions Emmy, Webby, Shorty, and a `Cannes Silver Lion nomination` across shared work, but does not reliably assign those honors to individual current cases. Verify project, year, category and exact Cannes status before displaying them.

# Case-by-case metric targets + copy audit

## 01 Nike SB × Staple - Panda Pigeon

### Best proof to retrieve

Primary:
- `XXK` community votes / participants in the colorway choice.
- `XX min` to sell out, or `100%` sell-through in the release window.

Secondary:
- `XXM` earned/organic reach or media impressions.
- resale premium / secondary-market demand only if there is a clean, attributable number.

Why these fit: the idea was community choice followed by a high-demand drop. Participation + sell-through proves the idea better than generic impressions.

### Copy audit

Keep the basic tension, but make the assignment less theatrical and more precise.

Current headline is memorable but slightly assumes Nike literally asked whether another riot would happen.

Recommended first-screen line:
`A legend came back. Nostalgia was not enough.`

Recommended idea:
`Let the streets pick the shoe.`

Recommended problem:
`The original Pigeon Dunk had become sneaker mythology. A reissue could not survive on nostalgia alone, especially with the community that made it matter in the first place.`

Recommended move:
`Three sample colorways went into the sneaker community. The black-and-white won. The launch then used the original Chinatown story and 2005 newspaper coverage as history, not costume.`

No slop words: avoid `iconic`, `culture-defining`, `authentic`, `community-first` unless evidence earns them.

## 02 Virgin Galactic - Unity 22

### Existing strong proof

- `19M+` total viewers across livestream and linear.
- nearly `1M` new followers across major platforms in the pre-launch window.
- `70.7M` Facebook reach.

Potential additional business signal to retrieve:
- reservation/waitlist/sign-up lift during launch window, if the team tracked it.

Why these fit: this was a one-time live attention problem. Live audience + audience growth are the correct result family.

### Copy audit

Current headline introduces `five million` while the actual proof is 19M+, which creates needless cognitive friction.

Recommended headline:
`One launch. One chance to make the world care at the same time.`

Recommended problem:
`The first fully crewed Virgin Galactic spaceflight would happen once. The communications job was to turn a technical milestone into a shared live event.`

Recommended move:
`We built the pre-launch voice around collective participation, then carried it through countdown content, social and livestream promotion.`

Keep `Boldly/Together.` as the idea if source provenance remains clean.

## 03 Porsche × Lucasfilm - The Designer Alliance

### Existing strong proof

- `1.5m` physical S-91x model.
- unveiled alongside `The Rise of Skywalker` world premiere.

### Best missing proof to retrieve

Primary:
- `XXM` earned-media / social reach generated by the collaboration.
- `XXM` campaign-film / design-film views, if tracked.

Secondary:
- `XX` countries/markets or major press placements covering the S-91x.
- exact Cannes / Webby / Emmy / other award status only if this project can be matched to the source record.

Why these fit: the creative achievement is already obvious from the artifact. The missing recruiter proof is how far that artifact traveled culturally.

### Copy audit

Headline is strong: `Porsche built a spaceship.` Keep.

Cut strategy-deck language such as `adjacency`.

Recommended problem:
`Most brand collaborations borrow attention. Porsche needed to earn a place inside Star Wars.`

Recommended move:
`Porsche designers and Lucasfilm starship artists designed an actual spacecraft together. The campaign documented the collaboration and unveiled the finished S-91x at the world premiere.`

Recommended idea:
`Do not advertise next to Star Wars. Build something in it.`

## 04 Selsun Blue - Dan Driff

- `+XX pts` brand consideration or purchase intent.

## 06 TE Connectivity × Alita - The Science Behind Science Fiction

### Existing strong proof

### Best proof to retrieve

Primary:
- `XXK/XXM` qualified views of engineering/creator content.
- `+XX%` engagement or completion vs TE's normal B2B benchmark.

If the campaign was tied to demand generation:
- `XXK` qualified site visits.
- `XX` leads / MQLs / engineering-content conversions.

Why these fit: this was B2B creative aimed at technical audiences. Qualified engagement is more persuasive than broad consumer reach.

### Copy audit

Current headline is long and puts the joke before the assignment.

Recommended headline:
`Make a cyborg credible to people who build robots for a living.`

`Alita was Hollywood science fiction. TE Connectivity needed the partnership to mean something to engineers who spend their lives separating fiction from what can actually be built.`

## 07 Jose Cuervo - Playamar + Tradicional Cristalino

### Best proof to retrieve

This case needs launch-specific proof, ideally one signal per chapter.

Playamar candidates:
- `XXK` product trials / samples.
- `XXM` launch reach or earned impressions.
- `+XX pts` awareness/consideration among target drinkers.
- `+XX%` sell-through / velocity vs launch benchmark if available.

Cristalino candidates:
- `XX` retail/on-premise doors at launch.
- `+XX pts` consideration / intent.
- `XXM` reach or video views.
- `+XX%` trial, sell-through or velocity.

Why these fit: product launches need evidence of trial, adoption or movement in perception. `250 years` and `2 launches` are context, not results.

### Copy audit

The revised governing line is good:
`Make 250 years feel current.`

Current kicker `The job was not to make Cuervo act younger` is useful but can be compressed.

`Two launches. One old name. Make the behavior new, not the brand.`

Recommended assignment:
`Launch two new products in categories obsessed with whatever is newest in the room.`

Recommended governing idea:
`Use the authority Cuervo already had. Change the pace, format and occasion around it.`

Keep `Nobody talks about the 250-year-old at the party.` as a later authored beat, not the first-screen thesis.

## 10 SCOOBA LOVE - internal creative-evaluation system

### Context numbers we already have

- `3` documented core collaborators.

These are not results.

### Best proof to retrieve

Secondary:

`The point was not to make creativity mathematical. It was to make the argument around an idea clearer.`

# Visual proof system

## First screen

Order:

1. Client/project.
2. One governing headline/idea.
3. Ian's role.
4. One or two proof signals.
5. Dominant work image.

Proof signals should not be little dashboard cards. They are editorial interruptions: large figure or short award phrase, thin rule, restrained label.

## Awards treatment

Recommended component:

`HONORS` small mono label
`CANNES LIONS · SHORTLIST · 20XX · [CATEGORY]` readable sans/mono hybrid

No ribbon, medallion, trophy icon, laurel wreath, shiny gold or Behance-style badge.

Use muted warm metal/dirty-cream rather than a new award color.

## Image rhythm

The scattered-image problem comes from treating every asset as an independent slot.

New rule:
- one dominant hero image;
- then one consistent evidence spine below it;
- full-width image or intentional 2-up pair, never arbitrary floating sizes;
- one baseline/grid system per case;
- use darkness as an edit between image beats;
- do not repeat an image just to fill a section;
- if only one good support image exists, show one good image.

A case should feel cut like a film or magazine feature, not populated like a CMS.

# Sources informing the metric logic

- Cannes Lions Awards guide: measurable real-world impact and verifiable evidence matter; official status language is Shortlist / Bronze / Silver / Gold / Grand Prix.
- Sprout Social 2026 campaign guidance: match metrics to objective - awareness uses reach/impressions/share of voice, engagement uses interaction metrics, conversion uses leads/downloads/conversions.
- HubSpot campaign measurement guidance: product launches favor sales/pre-orders/adoption; awareness favors sentiment/social/press mentions; engagement favors shares/interactions.
- NN/g hiring research: hiring managers value outcomes, business impact, explicit ownership and evidence that the candidate knows what to measure.
- AIGA/portfolio-review guidance: be ready to explain role, result/ROI and publicity/business effect.
