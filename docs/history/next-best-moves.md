# PZ Portfolio / Last Stop Diner — Next Best Moves (v1, for Whetstone review)

## Frame
Landing is locked: blunt diner exterior. IAN LUNA cream lightbox above, ABOUT amber neon left, WORK huge green in the front windows, HEARSAY on the red door right, dark car lower-left, wet reflective ground.

/work is the reverse angle: seated at booth height inside the same diner, looking straight through the front plate glass at an ordinary wet street. Selected case studies live on physical cream sign cabinets across the road, borrowing material DNA from the IAN LUNA lightbox. Head-on branch is active. Diagonal highway / billboard-field branch is retired (broke the diner's architecture).

Case titles (Beast Games / Go / Hometown / The Quiet Issue) are PLACEHOLDERS. Real case studies get written later. The build must not depend on final copy.

## Move 1 (blocking) — Lock a clean head-on background plate
Regenerate from the latest head-on frame as a background plate only:
- Camera: booth height, straight through the front plate glass. Same frontal logic as the landing.
- Foreground: oxblood booth lower-left, tabletop with mug, salt and pepper, napkin holder as anchor. One pendant lamp top-left. Bottom-left stays dark and clear for a live nav overlay.
- Window: 3 to 4 panes, black mullions positioned to fall between sign slots, never across a sign face.
- Exterior: ordinary wet street, practical light only (streetlamp, sign lamps), flat black night sky, dead dark areas encouraged, minimal world clutter.
- Sign cabinets: BLANK cream cabinets only. No type, numbers, networks, or icons. Four cabinets, unevenly placed, at varied depth and size, Beast slot largest and most central, every cabinet fully clear of mullions.
- Contamination: faint non-letter green on glass, sill, vinyl, tabletop only. No letterforms.
- Forbidden: readable WORK, any W/O/word fragment, reflected green type, baked nav, blocked titles, highway, desert, dramatic sky, billboard field.

## Move 3 — Build the soft-lock nav live in HTML/CSS

## Move 4 — Treatment layers
Sign grime, lamp falloff, acrylic yellowing, glass dirt, faint non-letter green contamination, mild halation, subtle grain. Flaws in objects and materials, never in usability.

## Move 5 — Interactions
Hover identifies only. Click promotes to hero. Keyboard arrows cycle, Enter opens active case. Reduced motion gets simple fades.

## Move 6 (last) — Landing-to-work transition
Threshold, not title sequence: click WORK, small sign-voltage flicker, old door chime, exposure handoff, inside view. Under ~1.2s. Sound is proof of crossing, not a gag.

## Open decision
Base frame for the blank plate: the latest render (darker, Muller-correct, no baked nav — recommended) versus the previous iteration (warmer interior, more residential clutter). Recommendation: latest as the base, warmed one notch.

## Governing laws applied
Specific before polished. Cinematic means observed, not glossy. Weirdness needs rules (work posted outside as local signage). One correction beats one hundred additions (lock the plate, do not add atmosphere). Behavioral truth over abstract concept. Fix toward truer, not safer.
